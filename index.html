ABOUT NCUBEFORGES LATERITE

A local idea with
a solid foundation.

NCUBEFORGES is a proposed laterite block extraction and supply business based in Pawas, Ratnagiri, Maharashtra. The idea is to connect the region’s laterite resources with the practical needs of builders, contractors and individual property owners.

Our approach centres on understanding each order, communicating specifications clearly and planning supply responsibly. Before any extraction activity, all applicable permissions, environmental requirements and safety measures must be confirmed.
